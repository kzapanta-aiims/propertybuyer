Why use a buyer's agent in Adelaide?

A buyer's agent in Adelaide acts only for you, so the selling agent never learns your budget, your deadline or your finance position.

Go direct and you hand all three to the person paid to act against you. Adelaide adds a second problem: interstate buyers and investors now compete for the same tightly held homes, so the good ones sell quickly and often quietly.

  • Your position stays private until the contract is signed.
  • We appraise before you fall in love, so the number is set by evidence.
  • At auction we bid to a walk-away figure agreed with you the day before.

What is an off-market property in Adelaide?

Off market means a property offered to buyers' agents before or instead of a public listing, and Propertybuyer's brief goes to a database of more than 10,000 selling agents.

It is access, not a discount. Off-market stock often carries a premium because the seller is trading certainty for price, so the job is to see it early and price it correctly, not to assume it is a bargain.

What is the difference between a buyer's agent and a buyer's advocate?

Nothing. A buyer's advocate and a buyer's agent are the same licensed role acting for the buyer only. South Australia uses both terms; New South Wales and Queensland mostly say agent.
