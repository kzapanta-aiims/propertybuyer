Questions
before you brief us

Do you only buy homes?

No. We buy homes, investment properties, commercial assets and development sites, always for the buyer and never for the seller. Your brief goes to a buyer's agent who works that kind of purchase.

What does it cost?

We charge a fixed fee of 1.5 to 2 percent of the purchase price for a full search. If you have already found the property, appraise and negotiate is 1 percent. The first conversation costs nothing.

How long does it usually take?

Most clients go from engagement to purchase in 30 to 60 days. A tight brief in a liquid market can be faster; a rare brief takes as long as it takes to find the right property.

Does the seller pay you anything?

No. We take no commission from sellers, developers or agents. You are the only person paying us, so you are the only person we answer to.

What if I have already found a property?

Then the search is done and the risk is not. We inspect it, tell you what it is worth, and negotiate or bid so you do not overpay.
